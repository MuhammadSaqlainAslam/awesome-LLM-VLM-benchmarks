# MedHopQA: A Disease-Centered Multi-Hop Reasoning Benchmark for Biomedical LLM QA (2026)

## Problem
Biomedical QA benchmarks suffer from three compounding failure modes: multiple-choice formats allow models to succeed through answer elimination rather than genuine reasoning; benchmark performance saturates as frontier models approach ceiling scores; and training data contamination undermines score validity. Existing biomedical benchmarks also underrepresent the multi-hop reasoning that genuine clinical synthesis requires — determining a diagnosis or treatment plan typically requires integrating information from multiple distinct knowledge sources, not retrieving a single fact. A benchmark that enforces multi-hop reasoning, resists saturation, and tests contamination robustness simultaneously has not previously existed.

## Method
**MedHopQA** (arXiv: 2605.12361, May 2026; BioCreative IX track) introduces a disease-centered benchmark constructed through a structured pipeline combining **human annotation, triage, iterative verification, and LLM-as-a-judge validation**. Each question requires synthesising information across **two distinct Wikipedia articles** with open-ended free-text answers (no multiple-choice). Annotations are augmented with **ontology-grounded synonym sets** from MONDO, NCBI Gene, and NCBI Taxonomy to ensure consistent entity linking across answer variations. The benchmark comprises **10,000 total questions** (1,000 scored; 9,000 withheld on CodaBench) and is designed around three core constraints: compositional reasoning, saturation resistance, and contamination resistance.

## Benchmarks / Datasets
- 10,000 total question-answer pairs (1,000 scored; 9,000 held out on CodaBench)
- Disease-centered multi-hop reasoning (2-article synthesis required)
- Open-ended free-text answers (no multiple-choice, no answer elimination)
- MONDO + NCBI Gene + NCBI Taxonomy ontology-grounded synonym sets
- BioCreative IX shared task track

## Key Results

| Design Property | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Multi-hop synthesis required | 2 distinct Wikipedia articles per question |
| Answer format | Open-ended free text (no elimination shortcut) |
| Saturation resistance | 9,000 withheld answers on CodaBench |
| Contamination resistance | Compositional reasoning + ontology grounding |

- **MedHopQA enforces genuine multi-hop synthesis — correct answers cannot be derived from either source article alone, preventing single-document recall masquerading as reasoning**
- Open-ended free-text format eliminates the answer-elimination shortcut endemic to multiple-choice biomedical benchmarks, providing a more conservative and reliable measure of LLM biomedical reasoning
- Saturation resistance is structurally guaranteed by withholding 9,000 answers on CodaBench — publicly available answers (1,000) allow development while 9,000 prevent benchmark gaming
- Ontology-grounded synonym sets from MONDO, NCBI Gene, and NCBI Taxonomy ensure answer evaluation correctly handles synonym variation without requiring exact string matching

## Enterprise / Industry Relevance
Foxconn operates Foxconn Health (workforce health management) and is exploring biomedical AI applications for its 800,000+ employee health programme. MedHopQA is directly relevant as a pre-deployment evaluation tool for any FoxBrain component performing biomedical reasoning — medication interaction synthesis, disease risk summarisation, occupational health assessment — where multi-hop synthesis from multiple knowledge sources is required. For FoxBrain in biomedical contexts, MedHopQA provides a saturation-resistant evaluation protocol that can track genuine capability improvement as models are updated, rather than saturating as current multiple-choice benchmarks do. The CodaBench leaderboard provides a standardised external comparison point for FoxBrain biomedical reasoning capability against peer systems.
