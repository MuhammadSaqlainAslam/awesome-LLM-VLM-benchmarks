# SEQUOR: A Multi-Turn Benchmark for Realistic Constraint Following in Extended Conversations (2026)

## Problem
Single-turn instruction following benchmarks measure whether a model complies with constraints in an isolated prompt. Enterprise AI assistants operate in extended multi-turn conversations where instructions evolve: users add new constraints, refine earlier ones, and occasionally contradict previous directives. No benchmark had measured whether models maintain constraint compliance across realistic multi-turn dialogues where instructions change dynamically — leaving a critical evaluation blind spot for deployed conversational assistants.

## Method
**SEQUOR** (arXiv: 2605.06353, May 2026) introduces an automated multi-turn evaluation framework featuring **persona-driven simulated user interactions** with constraints extracted from authentic user conversations. The benchmark introduces constraints at varying points in the dialogue — beginning, middle, and late — and measures compliance under three conditions: (1) single constraint tracked over a growing conversation, (2) multiple simultaneous constraints, and (3) constraints that are added or replaced at arbitrary conversation points. Instruction-following accuracy is measured at each turn to track degradation trajectories.

## Benchmarks / Datasets
- Persona-driven simulated multi-turn interactions
- Constraints extracted from authentic user conversations
- Three evaluation conditions: single / multiple / dynamically updated constraints
- Constraint introduction at arbitrary dialogue positions (beginning / middle / late)
- Instruction-following accuracy measured at each turn

## Key Results

| Condition | Accuracy Drop |
|---|---|
| Single constraint over longer conversation | **>11% drop** |
| Multiple simultaneous constraints | **>40% drop** |
| Adding or replacing constraints mid-conversation | **>9% drop** |

- **Multi-turn constraint compliance drops exceed 11% for single constraints as conversations lengthen — models progressively forget or override earlier user instructions**
- **Multiple simultaneous constraints cause >40% accuracy reduction** — models cannot reliably maintain a complex constraint set across a conversation
- Adding or replacing constraints at arbitrary positions reduces accuracy by more than 9% — constraint updates are not reliably integrated into ongoing compliance
- Persona-driven simulation with authentic constraint sources reveals that real-user instruction evolution is significantly more challenging than synthetic benchmark conditions

## Enterprise / Industry Relevance
FoxBrain's enterprise conversational deployments — procurement assistants, engineering support chatbots, and HR policy advisors — are used in extended multi-turn sessions where users frequently add constraints ("also make sure it complies with ISO 9001"), refine earlier instructions, and contradict previous directions. SEQUOR's finding that compliance drops exceed 40% under multiple simultaneous constraints means FoxBrain's assistants in these contexts are likely violating user-specified constraints on more than 40% of constrained turns in a realistic session. For Foxconn's compliance-sensitive workflows where instruction adherence is auditable, this is a systemic reliability failure. SEQUOR's methodology — tracking per-turn constraint compliance trajectories — should be adopted as a FoxBrain evaluation protocol for any conversational assistant operating in regulated or compliance-sensitive contexts before production deployment.
