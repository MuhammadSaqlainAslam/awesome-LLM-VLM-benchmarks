# X-PCR: A Benchmark for Cross-modality Progressive Clinical Reasoning in Ophthalmic Diagnosis (2026)

## Problem
Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) are being evaluated for clinical deployment in ophthalmology, yet existing benchmarks treat diagnosis as a single-step classification task — ignoring the sequential, multi-modal reasoning that characterises real clinical workflows. No benchmark had assessed whether MLLMs can perform the full six-stage diagnostic chain (from image quality assessment through treatment planning) across the multiple imaging modalities (fundus photography, OCT, fluorescein angiography, etc.) that ophthalmologists integrate in practice.

## Method
**X-PCR** (arXiv: 2604.20350, April 23, 2026) is a benchmark for evaluating cross-modality progressive clinical reasoning in ophthalmic diagnosis. It comprises approximately 26,415 images and 177,868 expert-verified visual question-answering pairs drawn from 51 public datasets, covering 52 ophthalmic diseases. The benchmark evaluates two key capabilities: progressive reasoning through a six-stage diagnostic workflow, and cross-modality reasoning integrating six distinct imaging modalities. A total of 21 MLLMs are benchmarked.

## Benchmarks / Datasets
- ~26,415 images sourced from 51 public ophthalmic datasets
- 177,868 expert-verified VQA pairs covering 52 ophthalmic diseases
- Six-stage progressive reasoning chain: image quality assessment → lesion detection → disease identification → severity grading → differential diagnosis → clinical decision-making
- Six imaging modalities: fundus photography, OCT, fluorescein angiography, slit-lamp, visual field, ultrasound
- 21 MLLMs evaluated across both progressive and cross-modality tasks

## Key Results

| Evaluation Dimension | Key Finding |
|---|---|
| Progressive reasoning (6-stage chain) | Critical gaps in sequential logic across all 21 MLLMs |
| Cross-modality integration | Significant failures in synthesising multi-modal evidence |
| Overall MLLM clinical readiness | No model approaches specialist-level performance |
| Stage-level analysis | Later reasoning stages (differential / decision-making) show steepest degradation |

- **All 21 evaluated MLLMs show critical gaps in both progressive reasoning and cross-modal integration — no model approaches specialist-level ophthalmic diagnostic capability**
- Performance degrades significantly across the six reasoning stages, with the sharpest drops at differential diagnosis and clinical decision-making — the stages requiring synthesis rather than perception
- Cross-modality integration (combining evidence from fundus + OCT + other modalities) reveals failure modes invisible to single-modality benchmarks
- The 177,868-pair scale and expert verification make X-PCR the largest rigorously curated ophthalmic MLLM evaluation to date

## Enterprise / Industry Relevance
Foxconn's healthcare technology division and its investments in medical device manufacturing make ophthalmic AI a relevant deployment target. X-PCR's six-stage progressive reasoning framework mirrors the quality-control inspection chains used in Foxconn's manufacturing processes — where an agent must progress from raw sensor data quality checks through anomaly detection to a final go/no-go decision. The benchmark's cross-modality design also parallels Foxconn's multi-sensor inspection environments (visual, infrared, ultrasonic), where FoxBrain must integrate heterogeneous data streams. Tracking FoxBrain performance on X-PCR informs both healthcare AI deployments and the broader multi-modal reasoning capability required for factory automation.
