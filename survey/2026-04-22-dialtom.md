# DialToM: A Theory of Mind Benchmark for Forecasting State-Driven Dialogue Trajectories (2026)

## Problem
Despite widespread claims that LLMs possess Theory of Mind (ToM), it remains unclear whether this reflects genuine reasoning or pattern-matching on surface cues. Existing ToM evaluations focus narrowly on mental-state identification, omitting whether models can actually *use* those inferences to predict real-world social outcomes. DialToM fills this gap by pairing mental-state prediction with prospective diagnostic forecasting of dialogue trajectories.

## Method
**DialToM** (arXiv: 2604.20443, April 22, 2026) is a human-verified, multiple-choice benchmark built from 5,943 natural conversation contexts spanning three domains. It evaluates two complementary ToM dimensions: Literal ToM (retrospective mental-state prediction) and Functional ToM (prospective diagnostic forecasting — identifying state-consistent dialogue continuations solely from mental-state profiles). The full dataset contains ~41,601 questions (35,658 retrospective, 5,943 forecasting).

Authors: Neemesh Yadav, Palakorn Achananuparp, Jing Jiang, Ee-Peng Lim

## Benchmarks / Datasets
- **Scale:** 5,943 conversation contexts; ~41,601 total multiple-choice questions
- **Tasks/Tracks:** Literal ToM (retrospective mental-state inference), Functional ToM (prospective diagnostic forecasting)
- **Models evaluated:** 13 LLMs — Gemini 3 Pro, Gemini 2.5 Pro/Flash, GPT-4.1, GPT-5, Llama 8B/70B/4 Maverick, Mistral Nemo/24B, Qwen 3 235B, DeepSeek V3, Kimi K2, GPT-OSS 120B
- **Metrics:** Accuracy on Literal ToM and Functional ToM tasks; human baseline comparison

## Key Results

| Model | Literal ToM Accuracy | Functional ToM Accuracy |
|---|---|---|
| Gemini 3 Pro | ~98% | ~83–87% |
| GPT-5 | ~93–95% | ~14–17% |
| Most other LLMs | ≥80% | ≤25% (near random) |
| Human baseline | 24–32% | — |

- **Critical reasoning asymmetry: most LLMs score ≥80% on mental-state identification but collapse to ≤25% (near-random) on forecasting dialogue trajectories from those states.**
- Only Gemini 3 Pro maintained strong Functional ToM (~83–87%), while GPT-5 fell from 93–95% to 14–17% — highlighting that model size alone does not guarantee social reasoning capability.
- Weak semantic alignment was found between human and LLM-generated mental-state inferences, suggesting LLMs build different internal representations of social context.

## FoxBrain Relevance
Enterprise AI assistants deployed in Foxconn manufacturing and supply-chain settings frequently operate in multi-party dialogue environments — coordinating between production planners, logistics teams, and quality engineers. DialToM's Functional ToM task directly probes whether a model can predict and steer conversation outcomes based on stakeholder mental states, a capability critical for negotiation support, escalation routing, and automated meeting facilitation. The benchmark's finding that most models fail at prospective reasoning despite strong retrospective accuracy counsels caution when deploying current LLMs in high-stakes inter-department coordination workflows. Foxconn's FoxBrain development roadmap should target Functional ToM as an explicit evaluation axis for enterprise dialogue agents.
