# Kimi-K2.6: Visual Agentic Intelligence at 1-Trillion-Parameter Scale (2026)

## Problem
Frontier agentic models face a compound challenge: they must simultaneously support long-context reasoning, multi-modal visual input, code generation, and orchestrating hundreds of sub-agents — while remaining deployable within practical compute budgets. No prior open-weight model had demonstrated competitive performance against the leading proprietary frontier models across agentic, coding, reasoning, and vision benchmarks simultaneously. Moonshot AI built Kimi-K2.6 to close this gap with a 1T-parameter MoE architecture that activates only 32B parameters per token.

## Method
**Kimi-K2.6** (Moonshot AI, April 2026; arXiv: 2602.02276) is a 1-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts multimodal model with 32B activated parameters per token. Key architectural features include:
- **MLA (Multi-head Latent Attention):** Reduces KV cache overhead for long-context inference
- **MoonViT (400M):** Dedicated vision encoder for image and experimental video understanding
- **384 experts, 8 selected per token** plus 1 shared expert, 61 total layers
- **256K native context window**
- **Three reasoning modes:** Thinking (default, full chain-of-thought), Instant (fast, no reasoning), Preserve Thinking (retains reasoning across multi-turn conversations for agent efficiency)
- **Native INT4 quantisation** for memory-efficient deployment
- **Agent swarm architecture:** Scales to 300 sub-agents with 4,000+ coordinated execution steps
- **Modified MIT licence**

## Key Results

- **54.0 on HLE-Full with tools — Kimi-K2.6 beats GPT-5.4 (52.1) on the Humanity's Last Exam agentic benchmark, establishing it as the top-performing open-weight model on the hardest general reasoning evaluation**
- **SWE-Bench Pro 58.6 — best reported score on the harder SWE-Bench Pro variant**, ahead of GPT-5.4 (57.7) and Claude Opus 4.6 (53.4)
- 96.4 on AIME 2026 and 90.5 on GPQA-Diamond confirm frontier-tier mathematical and scientific reasoning
- DeepSearchQA F1 of 92.5 — 13.9 points ahead of GPT-5.4 (78.6) on deep web research tasks
- Agent swarm capability (300 sub-agents, 4,000+ steps) enables autonomous long-horizon workflows without human supervision
- Experimental video understanding capability adds a modality not present in most open-weight frontier models
- 1T parameters with 32B active per token — same inference cost profile as a 32B dense model at full capability
